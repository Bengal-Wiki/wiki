At **Bengal Wiki**, we believe that privacy is a fundamental human right. This Privacy Policy details how the Bengal Wiki Foundation handles, stores, and protects any user data gathered during your visits to our site.

## 1. Our Commitment to Privacy

We operate under the core principle of **minimal data collection**:
* We do not require you to create an account to read or browse articles.
* We do not track your browsing history across the web or sell your browsing habits to third-party advertisers.
* We do not use commercial trackers, spy scripts, or complex tracking beacons.

## 2. Information We Receive Automatically

When you access Bengal Wiki, we automatically receive certain basic metadata from your web browser, which is standard across all internet servers:
* **IP Address**: Temporarily used in server logs to prevent network abuse, coordinate security, and geolocate server load.
* **User-Agent string**: Details of your web browser and operating system so that our servers can optimize standard layouts and deliver high-performance responsive styling.
* **Referrer URL**: The page you visited prior to clicking a link to Bengal Wiki, utilized solely to measure traffic sources.

## 3. Server Logs Policy

Our system logs are kept strictly secure. They are automatically deleted or anonymized after **30 days**. We do not retain server logs or link them to any personal identifications.

## 4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use lightweight cookies and browser Local Storage solely to enhance your interactive experiences:
* **Search Cache**: Local Storage is utilized to cache search metrics so that autocomplete recommendations appear instantly as you type.
* **Preferences**: Storing UI preferences such as font scaling or sidebar toggles.

For more details, please review our [Cookie statement](/cookie-statement).
