**Bengal Wiki** is a free, collaborative, premium digital encyclopedia dedicated to mapping and preserving the rich history, vibrant culture, outstanding personalities, and remarkable places of the Bengal region.

---

## Our Mission

Our mission is to build the most comprehensive, visually stunning, and intellectually robust repository of knowledge about Bengal. We strive to provide:
1. **Unbiased Knowledge**: Objective, academic-grade accounts of history, historical personalities, and places.
2. **Premium Visuals**: Reshaping classical information with modern aesthetics, HSL styling systems, responsive cards, and dynamic vector placeholders rather than dry text blocks.
3. **Accessibility**: Delivering content in high-performance web pages that load instantly and are highly readable across all platforms.

## Projects in Bengal Wiki

The encyclopedia is divided into distinct portals for structured browsing:
* **[Eminent People](/people)**: Profiles of Bengal's outstanding poets, reformers, political leaders, scientists, and social icons.
* **[Prominent Places](/places)**: A guide to geographic and architectural gems, ranging from terracotta temples and mountains to coastal mangrove deltas.
* **[Legendary Songs](/songs)**: Comprehensive reviews of historical national anthems, patriotic songs, and folk music.

## Technical Framework

Bengal Wiki is built using modern, light-weight, cutting-edge web technologies:
* **Core**: [Astro Framework](https://astro.build) (delivering zero-javascript static HTML by default).
* **Styling**: Vanilla HSL-based CSS variables, ensuring light-weight loading and visual harmony.
* **Database**: Decentralized Markdown files, making the content highly portable, readable, and easy to edit or expand.
