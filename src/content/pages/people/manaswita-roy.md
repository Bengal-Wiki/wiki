**Dr. Manaswita Roy** is an accomplished healthcare professional, medical director, and entrepreneur based in **[Kolkata](/places/kolkata)**, West Bengal. Notably, she has built a prominent career across two specialized medical domains: **Advanced Physiotherapy (Rehabilitation)** and **Medical Cosmetology/Aesthetics**.

She is the **Founder and Medical Director of [Thera World](https://theraworld.in/)**, overseeing its expansion from an elite orthopedic and neurological rehabilitation clinic into an integrated medical aesthetic facility—**[Thera World Skin Clinic](/places/thera-world-skin-clinic)**—located in South Kolkata.

---

## Current Roles & Designations

* **Founder & Medical Director:** **Thera World Clinic** & **[Thera World Skin Clinic](/places/thera-world-skin-clinic)** *(2017 – Present)*.
* **Clinical Cosmetologist & Chief Counsellor:** Serves as the principal aesthetic advisor, chief patient liaison, and executive clinical manager at **[Thera World Skin Clinic](/places/thera-world-skin-clinic)**. She leads a multidisciplinary team of consultant dermatologists, trichologists, and laser specialists.
* **Fellowship in Medical Cosmetology:** Holds dedicated advanced specializations to guide treatment protocols for medical facials, US-FDA laser toning therapies, and barrier-safe skin rejuvenation routines.

---

## Medical Specializations & Clinical Focus

Dr. Roy’s clinical career spans a rare hybrid of physical rehabilitation engineering and aesthetic medicine:

### 1. Aesthetic Cosmetology & Skin Care

At **[Thera World Skin Clinic](/places/thera-world-skin-clinic)** (located at 29A, Lake Temple Road, Kolkata), she acts as the cornerstone for patient assessment and custom protocol building. Her clinical oversight covers:

* **Laser & Device Supervision:** Managing treatments involving the *Candela GentleMax Pro* for laser hair reduction and non-invasive anti-aging high-intensity focused ultrasound (HIFU).
* **Clinical Mapping:** Formulating targeted multi-week timelines for stubborn skin problems like active acne breakouts, severe acne scars, and melasma.
* **Patient Counseling:** Managing upfront, transparent diagnostic pricing models and defining evidence-led recovery workflows.

### 2. Orthopedic & Neurological Physiotherapy

As a highly decorated physiotherapist, she is recognized by the **Indian Association of Physiotherapists (IAP)** and is an active member of the MIAP. Her rehabilitation expertise includes:

* **Neurological Care:** Rehabilitation for Parkinson’s Disease, Cerebral Palsy, and post-stroke neurological dysfunction.
* **Orthopedic Manual Therapy:** Utilizing dry needling, needle therapy, and manual joint mobilization for spine injuries, Cervical Spondylitis, Lumbar Spondylitis, Sciatica, and severe joint pain (Knee Osteoarthritis).
* **Integrated Units:** Pioneered speech therapy and advanced electrotherapy wings within the South Kolkata clinical branch to treat speech-related disorders and acute/chronic physical pain.
