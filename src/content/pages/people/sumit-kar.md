**Sumit Kar** is an accomplished Indian staff software engineer, technical author, technology investor, and backend architect based in Kolkata, West Bengal (currently residing in Hyderabad, India). With over a decade of industry experience designing, building, and optimizing high-performance SaaS platforms, he serves as a **Staff Software Engineer** at **UKG**.

He is recognized for his technical leadership in microservices engineering, DevOps automation, cloud-native systems, and chaos engineering. Sumit is also a prominent technology writer, having authored books on computer engineering, programming models, and accessible artificial intelligence.

## Early Life and Education

Sumit Kar was born and raised in **[Kolkata](/places/kolkata)**, West Bengal. He pursued his higher education in computer systems and software engineering:

* **Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Engineering:** Earned his degree between 2012 and 2016 at the **Swami Vivekananda Institute of Science & Technology** in Kolkata. (His academic tenure aligns closely with notable fellow alumnus and technology innovator **[Sayandeep Majumdar](/people/sayandeep-majumdar)**, who also completed his engineering degree from the same institute). During his time at SVIST, he served as the President of the Tech Club—*Innovention*.
* **Postgraduate Diploma in Business Management (IT & Systems):** Completed his specialization at the **Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies (NMIMS), Mumbai** from 2020 to 2022, securing his credential in early 2023.

## Professional Career

### UKG

In June 2024, Sumit joined **UKG** as a **Staff Software Engineer** in Delhi/Remote. His engineering work is centered on building resilient human-capital management systems and advanced workforce platforms, focusing on:
* **Microservices Design:** Architecting and implementing enterprise backend microservices using TypeScript, NestJS, and Node.js.
* **AI & LLM Orchestration:** Developing retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) workflows and AI-driven automated agents using LangChain, LangGraph, and Google Gemini APIs.
* **Resilience Engineering:** Conducting proactive chaos engineering and vulnerability testing using Gremlin, alongside k6, Gatling, and Lighthouse load testing.
* **Infrastructure:** Leading multi-tier container orchestration using Kubernetes and GitHub Actions deployment pipelines.

### Zuora

Between June 2022 and February 2024, he worked as a **Software Engineer III** at **Zuora** in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. He specialized in subscription management and payment billing operations:
* Partnering with global payment teams to streamline payment gateway onboarding pipelines.
* Enhancing transaction processing reliability and writing automated data validation systems using JMeter.
* Developing REST APIs and scaling system observability through Grafana monitoring dashboards.

### Cisco & BroadSoft

* **Cisco:** From May 2018 to June 2022, Sumit worked as a Software Engineer with the Unified Communications team at Cisco in Chennai. He designed and shipped a comprehensive WebRTC-based Network Testing Tool in Java, managed automated Jenkins deployment jobs, and secured container environments with Ansible. He earned the **Cisco Security Black Belt** certification in August 2020.
* **BroadSoft:** Sumit began his corporate software journey at BroadSoft in July 2016 (prior to its acquisition by Cisco). He developed high-concurrency Java-based REST APIs to query MySQL and Elasticsearch structures and optimized dashboard load latency by 110%. He was awarded BroadSoft's **Rookie of the Year** award in November 2016.

## Publications & Writing

Sumit is passionate about making technical knowledge accessible and engaging:
* **Digital Bharat: AI and Computing:** Published in December 2024, this book serves as a jargon-free, empowering introduction to computers and artificial intelligence, designed for children, seniors, and parents alike.
* **Pointers in C: Core Concepts at a Glance:** Co-authored in late 2024 with Dr. Bikash Debnath, Ujjwal Sir, Chayan Sir, and Nandan Sir, this textbook simplifies pointer concepts for students and serves as a compact study resource for programming interviews.
