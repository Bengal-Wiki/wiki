**Dr. Madhurima Dasgupta** is an Indian academic, sociologist, and author based in **[Kolkata](/places/kolkata)**, West Bengal. She is widely recognized for her contributions to teaching, research, and academic publishing, particularly in the areas of sociology of gender, intimate human relationships, and social structures. She currently serves as a Post-doctoral Research Fellow at **Åbo Akademi University** (Finland), the Academic Coordinator & HOD of the Department of Sociology at the **Ajay Kumar Das Seva Institution & Research Centre (AKDSIRC)**, West Bengal (Associate & Collaborator with UGC approved Universities), and a Business Development Officer at the **Eudoxia Research Centre** under **Eudoxia Research University** (USA). She is also the former Head of the Department (HOD) of Gender Studies and Social Studies at **Ashadi Wisdom Campus** in Sri Lanka.

## Overview

Dr. Dasgupta has over a decade of teaching and research experience in prestigious institutions in India and abroad. Her scholarly work focuses on gender studies, masculinity, the sociology of the body, and contemporary social structures. 

As an author, she has written autobiographical and academic volumes that critically analyze sociological themes, gender relations, and the struggles of carving out an identity in academic fields that have historically been marginalized. In addition to her books, she serves as a reviewer and editorial board member for leading international academic publishers including **Springer** and **Taylor & Francis**.

## Early Life and Education

Dr. Dasgupta was raised in West Bengal, India. She pursued her entire higher education at **Jadavpur University**, one of India's premier institutions for social sciences:

* **Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Sociology:** Her doctoral research focused on gender studies, reflecting her long-standing commitment to understanding and unpacking the structural dynamics of gender in society. She broke glass ceilings by becoming the only Ph.D. holder in her family, choosing a non-traditional academic path in sociology against the conventions of her close kin.
* **Master of Philosophy (M.Phil.) in Sociology:** Conducted advanced research in social sciences and academic methodology at Jadavpur University.
* **Master of Arts (M.A.) in Sociology:** Completed her postgraduate studies with a focus on sociological theories and contemporary Indian society.

With more than a decade of experience in higher education, Dr. Dasgupta has taught and guided students at several noted universities:

## Research and Scholarly Contributions

Dr. Dasgupta's research program lies at the intersection of gender, social justice, and human relationships. Her primary research areas include:
* **Sociology of Gender and Masculinity:** Investigating how patriarchal structures construct male identity and performance.
* **Body and Society:** Analyzing how physical bodies are disciplined, commodified, and perceived across cultures.
* **Intimate Human Relationships:** Studying modern courtship, domestic structures, and shifting relationship patterns in contemporary society.

Dr. Dasgupta is an active participant in global academic dialogues. She has published numerous papers in peer-reviewed international journals and has been invited to present her research at various international conferences as a guest speaker and session chair. Most notably, her advanced research paper was selected for presentation at the prestigious **University of Edinburgh** (ranked 28th globally and 5th in the UK as per the QS World University Rankings 2026).

She also actively reviews scholarly papers, helping shape the quality of academic publishing with publishers such as Springer and Taylor & Francis.

## Books and Publications

Dr. Dasgupta is the author of key books that bridge academic sociology and narrative non-fiction:

### 1. *Daring to Swim Against the Tide*
* **Type:** Autobiography & Sociological Memoir
* **Themes:** Empowerment, agency, glass ceilings, academic marginalization, and institutional barriers.
* **Synopsis:** In her debut autobiography, Dr. Dasgupta chronicles her strenuous journey as a female sociologist. The book debunks the systemic challenges she faced from family and society during an era when Sociology was frequently dismissed as a marginalized or non-conventional discipline. She details the experience of being the sole Ph.D. holder in her kin, standing firm with the highest academic credentials, and eventually seeing her work recognized globally at the University of Edinburgh. *Daring to Swim Against the Tide* serves as a guide and inspiration for students and researchers striving to build their identity in their chosen field against stereotypical expectations.

### 2. *Understanding Gender: Meaning, Concepts & Key Theories*
* **Publisher:** VerseWave Publishing
* **Target Audience:** Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) students, researchers, scholars, and general readers.
* **Structure:** A comprehensive academic volume comprising 24 chapters written by distinguished scholars.
* **Scope:** 
  * **Core Concepts:** Sex vs. gender, masculinity and femininity, patriarchy, and intersectionality.
  * **Themes:** Tracing feminist theories from historical waves to modern discourse, performativity, queer studies, caste–gender intersections, corporate inclusivity, and women's empowerment.
  * **Approach:** Blends classical sociological theories with contemporary global debates, utilizing rich case studies and accessible, critical explanations.
  * **Purpose:** Serves as a vital multidisciplinary resource in Gender Studies, Women's Studies, and Sociology curricula to analyze gender relations and contribute to struggles for social justice.
