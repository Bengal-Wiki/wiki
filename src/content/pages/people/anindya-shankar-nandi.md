**Anindya Shankar Nandi** is an Indian botanist, ecological researcher, and environmental project management specialist based in **[Kolkata](/places/kolkata)**, West Bengal. With a strong academic background in plant sciences and a specialization in invasive plant ecology and ecosystem analysis, he has contributed to critical sustainability and ecological restoration projects, including dissertation research at the prestigious Banaras Hindu University (BHU) and environmental initiatives under the national Ganga Rejuvenation program in collaboration with GIZ-India.

## Overview

Anindya's research and professional work focus on understanding the complex dynamics of plant invasions, assessing environmental impacts, and formulating ecosystem restoration strategies. His core competencies span field-based ecological sampling, scientific illustration, data visualization, biodiversity conservation, and project management. He is also co-author of a significant academic publication analyzing the economic and socio-ecological impacts of invasive species on urban systems.

## Early Life and Education

Anindya Shankar Nandi was born and raised in **[Kolkata](/places/kolkata)**, West Bengal. He pursued his education in science, focusing deeply on botany and environmental systems:

* **MSc in Botany** (2022–2025): Pursued at the renowned **Banaras Hindu University (BHU)** in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, specializing in invasive plant ecology and ecosystem analysis. He graduated with an outstanding Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of **8.95 / 10**.
* **BSc in Botany** (2019–2022): Completed his undergraduate degree at the historic **Scottish Church College** (affiliated with Calcutta University) in Kolkata. He secured a first-class degree with a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of **8.736 / 10**.
* **Higher Secondary Education** (2018–2019): Studied in the science stream at **Apeejay School, Salt Lake, Kolkata** under the CBSE Board, achieving an overall percentage of **86.4%**.
* **Secondary School Exam** (2016–2017): Completed his schooling at **Apeejay School, Salt Lake, Kolkata** under the CBSE Board, securing an excellent GPA of **9.6 / 10**.

## Research and Work Experience

### GIZ-India
**Project Management Intern** (June–October 2025)

Anindya worked as a Project Management Intern at the **Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ) GmbH** in India. He was actively involved in environmental sustainability initiatives supporting the prestigious national **Ganga Rejuvenation program**. His responsibilities included:
* Conducting research on ecosystem restoration, river basin health, and pollution control strategies.
* Assisting in the coordination, planning, and execution of environmental sustainability projects.
* Supporting field-level data collection, data analysis, and the preparation of comprehensive technical reports.
* Coordinating communication and collaborating with diverse project stakeholders, technical experts, and project teams.

### Ecosystem-Analysis Laboratory, Banaras Hindu University
**Dissertation Researcher** (January–May 2025)

As part of his postgraduate program, Anindya conducted high-impact dissertation research on plant invasion dynamics in the Ecosystem-Analysis Laboratory under the Department of Botany at BHU. His work involved:
* Studying the ecological impacts, colonization patterns, and disruption dynamics of invasive plant species in native habitats.
* Conducting detailed field-based sampling, vegetation surveys, and literature-based ecological research.
* Analyzing ecosystem disruption patterns to formulate conservation and management models.
