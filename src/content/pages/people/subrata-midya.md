**Subrata Midya** is an Indian entrepreneur, digital marketer, web developer, and public speaker from West Bengal, India. He is best known as the Founder of **Think nXt Media** (officially registered as Think-nXt Digitech Media Solutions LLP), a 360-degree digital marketing, information technology, and public relations agency operating out of Kolkata and Paschim Medinipur.

## Early Life and Education

Midya was born in West Bengal, India. He completed his secondary schooling in the science stream at **Balichak B H Institution** between 2017 and 2019, graduating with a 79% score. 

He then pursued his technical education at **JIS College of Engineering**, earning a Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science and Engineering from 2019 to 2023. During his college years, he actively participated in national student initiatives, serving in the **National Cadet Corps (NCC)** with the *49 Bengal BN NCC* under the Kalyani Group.

## Career and Entrepreneurship

### Think nXt Media

In July 2021, while still a computer science engineering student, Midya founded **Think nXt Media**. His objective was to establish a unified agency framework that combined technical web infrastructure with marketing execution, serving clients globally.

On April 3, 2025, the company formally consolidated its corporate status by registering with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) as **Think-nXt Digitech Media Solutions LLP** under RoC-Kolkata. The LLP lists Subrata Midya and Neha Ghosh as its designated partners, with its primary corporate address situated in Hamirpur, Balichak (Paschim Medinipur).

The agency provides core operational services including:
* **Web & App Development**: UI/UX web design, e-commerce platforms, portfolio applications, and custom software. The firm also developed the productivity utility application *Notation - Remember Everything*, published on the Google Play Store.
* **Digital Marketing**: Targeted search engine marketing, Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn advertising frameworks.
* **Public Relations (PR) & Brand Strategy**: Organizing press releases, media distribution networks, and strategic communication consulting.

### Media Ventures

In March 2026, on the occasion of International Women's Day, Midya expanded into regional publishing by launching the first edition of **Entrepreneur Street Magazine** (also published under the brand *Entrepreneur Elite Magazine*). Titled *"Inspiring Women of West Bengal 2026"*, the inaugural publication highlighted notable regional female entrepreneurs and community advocates.

## Public Speaking and Professional Affiliations

Midya frequently serves as a panelist, pitch presenter, and guest speaker within the West Bengal entrepreneurial ecosystem:
* **Conclaves & Panels**: He was an invited panel speaker for the Calcutta Management Association's (CMA) *Union Budget 2026* discussion and attended the *News 18 Bangla Leadership Conclave 2026*.
* **Startup Pitching**: He delivered his agency's structural pitch at the Indian Statistical Institute (ISI) during the *MSME and Startup Conclave Event*.
* **Media Profiles**: He was featured as a live guest on TV9 Bangla during *The Spotlight Show* in early 2026.
* **Industry Training**: He participated in the *Meta Tech Provider Accelerator Programme* at Hyatt Kolkata to advance the integration of social media ad frameworks within agency workflows.
