**Sayandeep Majumdar** is an accomplished Indian senior full-stack software engineer, technical consultant, and product architect based in Kolkata, West Bengal. With over seven years of professional experience building, maintaining, and scaling cloud-native distributed systems, he currently serves as a **Senior Software Engineer** at **Volition LLP**.

Majumdar is recognized for his focus on practical execution, software reliability, and aligning technical choices with business outcomes. His clinical focus covers SaaS platforms, microservices architecture, fault-tolerant systems, and automated deployment pipelines.

Majumdar’s engineering philosophy relies on purpose-driven software architecture:
