**Tanisha Banerjee** is a prominent Indian self-publishing strategist, book cover designer, and author relations consultant from West Bengal, India. She is highly recognized for her work in the independent publishing ecosystem, helping emerging and established authors navigate the complexities of manuscript layouts, self-publishing distribution channels, brand design, and royalty structures.

Banerjee has built a reputation for providing complete, end-to-end publishing solutions that blend technical layout precision with creative artistic direction, allowing writers to successfully launch their works across global digital and print-on-demand marketplaces.

## Technical Contributions to Independent Publishing

### 1. Publishing Architecture & Typesetting
In her role as a self-publishing consultant, Banerjee specializes in preparing raw manuscripts for global distribution. She manages:
* **Typesetting and Layouts**: Structuring margins, page sizes, running headers, and drop caps to match international printing standards.
* **eBook Formats**: Converting Word files and PDFs into high-quality reflowable and fixed-layout EPUB formats, ensuring optimal reading on Kindle, Kobo, and Apple Books.
* **Cataloging**: Coordinating with official registration portals to secure **ISBN allotments** and generate bar codes, ensuring books are ready for retail catalog indexing.

### 2. Book Cover Design & Author Branding
Banerjee is renowned for her visual designs. She works closely with writers to translate thematic concepts and plots into striking, genre-specific **Book Cover Designs**. She covers both:
* **Wrap Designs**: Full layouts including front cover, spine, and back cover blurbs formatted for paperback and hardcover specifications.
* **Marketing Kits**: Creating promotional teasers, social media templates, and mockups to fuel pre-launch campaigns.

### 3. Royalty Strategy & Vendor Coordination
A critical element of her strategy involves setting up profitable independent models for authors. She consults on:
* **Royalty Structures**: Helping authors understand distribution fees, print costs, and retail percentages to optimize pricing strategies.
* **Global Vendors**: Navigating print-on-demand giants (such as Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, and Draft2Digital) and managing local print vendors for physical inventory requirements.
