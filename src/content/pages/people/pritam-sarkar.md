**Pritam Sarkar** is an Indian entrepreneur, startup founder, consultant, and technology professional based in **[Kolkata](/places/kolkata)**, West Bengal. He is the Director of **General Query** and is best known for co-founding **QueKey**, a passwordless facial authentication platform that aims to make digital login experiences simple, secure, and seamless.

## Overview

Pritam Sarkar works across technology development, startup growth, product strategy, digital identity, user experience (UX), and business development. His work focuses on building technology that simplifies everyday digital interactions rather than making them more complicated.

He is closely associated with **QueKey**, a facial authentication platform designed to remove the need for passwords and OTPs. The platform allows users to log in using face-based authentication and is positioned as a solution for businesses seeking a smoother and more secure authentication experience.

## Early Life and Education

Pritam Sarkar studied at **Techno Main Salt Lake**, where he completed a **Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) Honours** in Computer Programming between 2018 and 2021. He earlier completed his higher secondary education in the science stream at the historic **Berhampore J. N. Academy** in **[Murshidabad](/places/murshidabad)**.

His academic background in computer applications and programming helped shape his interest in digital products, startups, data science, authentication systems, and technology-led problem solving.

## Career and Startup Ventures

### General Query

Pritam Sarkar serves as the **Director** of **General Query**. He has been listed as Co-Founder of General Query since August 2024. His role includes:
* Defining long-term business strategy and leading fundraising efforts.
* Building key partnerships and overseeing operations.
* Driving go-to-market strategy and aligning product development with market requirements.

General Query functions as a parent brand for multiple technology products, including **QueKey**, **KeyCase**, and other upcoming products. The brand focuses on long-term technology architecture, digital identity, trust, and scalable product innovation.

### QueKey

**QueKey** is a passwordless facial authentication platform co-founded by **Pritam Sarkar** and **[Imanul Anam Siddique](/people/imanul-siddique)**. The platform was created to solve the problems associated with passwords and OTPs, including forgotten passwords, phishing risks, login delays, and poor user experience.

QueKey enables users to log in using facial authentication instead of traditional credentials. Its core idea is to make authentication feel invisible, secure, and effortless. According to Hustle Legends, QueKey uses AI-powered facial authentication and OAuth 2.0 integration to provide secure login experiences across devices and platforms.

The platform is positioned for use across industries such as banking, healthcare, SaaS, enterprise security, and other sectors where secure authentication is important.

#### Inspiration Behind QueKey

The idea for QueKey began when Pritam Sarkar noticed how frustrating password-based systems could be after a friend was locked out of an important account. This led him to question why digital login still depended so heavily on passwords and whether authentication could be made as simple as looking at a screen.

He later discussed the idea with **[Imanul Anam Siddique](/people/imanul-siddique)**, who had experience in building secure systems. Together, they began developing QueKey as a platform to make authentication faster, safer, and easier for users and businesses.

#### Startup Journey and Recognition

QueKey has been active in Kolkata’s startup ecosystem and has participated in major events such as **CCU Fest 2025** and **Startup Carnival 2026**. At **Startup Carnival 5** held at Offbeat CCU, QueKey was selected among 12 finalist startups and won the **Winner – Best Startup Award**.

QueKey has also received startup media coverage. Hustle Legends published an article titled *"The Journey to a Password-less Future"*, highlighting the story of Pritam Sarkar and **[Imanul Anam Siddique](/people/imanul-siddique)** and their work in building QueKey.
