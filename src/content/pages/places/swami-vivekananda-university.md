# Swami Vivekananda University

## Overview
Swami Vivekananda University (SVU) is a private university located in Barrackpore, Kolkata, West Bengal, India. Established in 2019, it offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate courses in engineering, management, agricultural science, journalism, allied health, and other disciplines.
